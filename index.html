FAQ

Stuff people ask us

Answers as straight as our squeegee lines.

Why do people call you the "hard-to-reach" guys?

Between rope-access certification and water-fed pure-water poles, height just isn't a factor for us. Stairwell glass three storeys up, skylights, atrium panes, poolside windows and awkward balconies — if another crew quoted around it, chances are we'll take it on.

What actually happens during a window clean?

Every visit covers glass inside and out, along with screens, frames, sills and tracks — no upsell required. To us that's just the job, not an add-on.

Is window cleaning the only service you offer?

It's what we're known for, but we'll also handle pressure washing — driveways, paths, render, roofs — and gutter clearing. Most clients bolt it onto a window visit, though we're happy to book it as its own job too.

Do I have to be there while you work?

No need, so long as we can get to the exterior. We'll get on with the job and send through before-and-after photos when it's done, no matter where you are.

Is the business insured?

Yes — every job is covered by public liability insurance, and anyone working at height has the proper training to back it up.

Which suburbs can you get to?

We regularly work across the Eastern Suburbs, Inner East, Bayside and Inner City, plus everywhere in between. If your area isn't obviously on that list, just message us your suburb and we'll let you know right away.
